Trail Tips for Dog Parents

Keep your adventure safe and fun for everyone

💧

Bring Plenty of Water

Dogs need roughly 1 oz of water per pound of body weight on active days.

💳

Pack a Poop Kit

Leave no trace. Bring biodegradable bags and pack out everything.

🦵

Check Their Paws

Inspect for cuts, ticks, and foxtails before and after each hike.

☀️

Mind the Heat

Hike early or late to avoid the hottest hours. If you're hot, your dog is hotter.

🧴

ID and Tags

Make sure your dog's collar has up-to-date ID tags and is microchipped.

🧹

Know the Rules

Check trail regulations — many require leashes or prohibit dogs in certain areas.
